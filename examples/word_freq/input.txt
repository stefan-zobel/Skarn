The lighthouse keeper climbed the stairs every evening. At the top of the stairs
the lamp waited, and the keeper cleaned the glass before the light was lit.

Ships passed the lighthouse all night. Some ships were small fishing boats, some
were cargo ships bound for the harbour, and one ship, the old ferry, passed twice.

In the morning the keeper wrote the ships into the log: the fishing boats, the
cargo ships, and the ferry. Then the keeper slept, and the lamp rested until evening.
